Remote control Harmoneasy

This page talks to Harmoneasy over audioBus Protocol without pairing. It plays notes and drums, moves controllers, runs the transport, loads presets, and presses, switches or sets every control Harmoneasy offers. Notes that Harmoneasy sends back through its audioBus Protocol output appear under Sent by Harmoneasy.
