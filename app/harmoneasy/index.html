Harmoneasy

What would you like to make music do?

Turn one note into a chord, stay in key while you improvise, turn a melody into a bass line, remix a music file, or get a group playing to the same clock.

You play or import notes. Harmoneasy changes their pitch, harmony or rhythm, then plays the result. A chain connects those steps. These starting points build one for you.

Try it with your computer or onscreen keyboard. You can connect MIDI instruments after starting.
